## Description (max 4000)
Total Form Fitness is a habits-first health app built with your coach — not just
another calorie counter. It helps you do the small things consistently, because
that's what actually changes your health.

BUILD HABITS THAT STICK
• Simple daily check-offs for the habits that matter — movement, sleep, hydration,
  mindfulness and more.
• Streaks, a progress calendar, and a forgiving "streak freeze" so one missed day
  never wipes out your momentum.
• Earn levels and badges as you show up. Progress you can feel.

FOOD MADE EASY
• Log by barcode scan, search, or a quick photo.
• Enter real portions — "1 egg," "1 slice," a palm, a cup — no food scale needed.
• See calories and macros fill up as you go, with a balanced-plate builder that
  teaches portions at a glance.

WATER & BODY
• One-tap hydration tracking with your own daily goal.
• Weigh-ins with a smart trend line, plus optional measurements and private
  progress photos.

COACHING BUILT IN
• Your coach sees your progress and guides your targets — personalized to you.
• Message your coach right in the app.
• Gentle, never-shaming nudges when life gets busy.

Total Form Fitness supports healthy habits and nutrition awareness. It is not a
medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## Screenshots — shot list

Apple requires **iPhone 6.7"** screenshots (**1290 × 2796 px**). Provide 3–6;
that same set covers the other sizes. Easiest capture: open the live site in
Chrome → DevTools device toolbar → set a custom 1290×2796 (or iPhone 15 Pro Max)
→ log in as a demo client → screenshot each screen. (Take them at 100% so the
pixels match; crop the browser chrome.)

Capture these, in this order (first three matter most — they show in search):

1. **Today screen** — habits checked off + the progress rings filling.
   Caption: "Small habits, every day."
2. **Food logging** — the add-food screen or a day's log with macros.
   Caption: "Log food in seconds."
3. **Plate builder** — the balanced plate with foods on it.
   Caption: "See a balanced plate."
4. **Progress** — the weight trend + streak/level.
   Caption: "Watch your progress climb."
5. **Coach messaging** — a thread with the coach.
   Caption: "Your coach, in your pocket."
6. **Achievements / level** — badges wall or the level HUD.
   Caption: "Earn every win."

Tips:
- Use a demo account with realistic (not empty) data so screens look alive.
- Put the caption text in a colored band above each screenshot (Apple allows
  framed/marketing screenshots). Optional but it lifts conversion.
- Keep them consistent — same device frame, same background.
